Privacy Policy

Go Healthy · Breathe free. Live better.

Effective date: 29 October 2026 · Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains how Go Healthy handles information when you use the app or website.

1. Information we handle

  • App settings and activity: language, smoking goals, usual cigarettes per day, default cigarette price, reminder preference, and entries you make such as smoking records, dates/times and prices.
  • Account information: if you create or sign in to an account, Google Firebase Authentication may process your email address, account identifier and authentication information. If you choose Google sign-in, Google may provide basic account information such as email address and display name.
  • Cloud backup: if cloud-sync is configured and used, selected app settings and records may be stored in Firebase Cloud Firestore and associated with your account.
  • Restricted admin access: the verified Go Healthy administrator (rohit.fcg123@gmail.com) can view registered account email/display name and smoking records that have synced to the cloud, for service support and app administration. The administrator cannot see records that remain only on your device.
  • Technical information: service providers may process limited technical/security data to operate authentication, prevent abuse and maintain reliability.

Go Healthy is not designed to collect precise location, contacts, SMS, call logs, microphone or camera data.

2. Storage

App data may be stored in local app/browser storage on your device. If you use cloud-sync, selected data may also be stored in Firebase services. Local and cloud data are separate; deleting local data does not necessarily delete cloud copies.

3. Use of information

We use information to show progress and statistics, apply settings and reminders, authenticate accounts, back up or restore data where enabled, and maintain security. We do not sell personal information and do not intend to use smoking records for advertising or medical diagnosis.

4. Service providers and sharing

We use Google Firebase for authentication and, when enabled, cloud storage. Google processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not otherwise share personal information except as needed to operate the service, when you direct us to, or when required by applicable law.

5. Your choices and deletion

  • You can change settings and use the app's export/local-data controls where available.
  • Signing out does not delete your account or cloud records.
  • To request deletion of your account and associated cloud data, follow the Account & Data Deletion instructions or email rohit.fcg123@gmail.com from the account email address with subject “Go Healthy account and data deletion”. We may ask for reasonable verification and will process requests subject to applicable legal obligations.
  • You may contact us to ask about or request correction/deletion of information or raise a privacy grievance.

6. Security and retention

We use security controls available in the app and service providers, but no system can be guaranteed completely secure. Information is retained as needed to provide the service or comply with law. Deletion may take reasonable time to propagate through backups.

7. Children

Go Healthy is not intended for children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us for review.

8. Health disclaimer

Go Healthy is a habit-tracking and general wellness tool, not a medical device or substitute for professional advice, diagnosis or treatment. Estimates and suggestions may be approximate.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or requirements change. For privacy questions or grievances, email rohit.fcg123@gmail.com.

This policy is intended to support transparency under applicable Indian data-protection requirements, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and applicable rules, and Google Play policies. It does not certify compliance by itself.
