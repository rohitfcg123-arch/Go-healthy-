Go Healthy: Account & Data Deletion

How to request deletion of your account and associated data

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the email address associated with your Go Healthy account.
  2. Use the subject “Go Healthy account and data deletion”.
  3. State that you want your account and associated cloud data deleted. We may ask for reasonable verification to protect your account.

Request deletion by email

What may be deleted

After verifying the request, we will process deletion of your Go Healthy account and associated cloud data under our control, subject to applicable law. Information that must be retained by law may be retained for the required period. Backups may take a reasonable time to reflect deletion.

Local data on your device

Local app/browser data may remain on your device even after a cloud-account deletion request. Use the app's available local-data deletion control, or clear the app/browser storage on your device. Export any records you wish to keep before deleting them.

Need help?

This page describes the current request process; it does not perform an automatic deletion when opened.
